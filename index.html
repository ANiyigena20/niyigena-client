Install in a few minutes

Install it on a PC at the same place as your cameras, and leave that PC on.

Download

Click Download for Windows and open Niyigena-Client-Setup.exe.

Install

Click Next through the installer. The app opens when it finishes.

Create your account

Set your name and password, then click Install video engine (one click).

Add cameras

Open Network Cameras — it finds the cameras on your Wi-Fi. Tick them and enter their login.

Windows may say "Windows protected your PC". That's normal for new apps that haven't built up a download history yet. Click More info, then Run anyway.

Requirements

 

Computer

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • 4 GB RAM (8 GB for 16+ cameras)
  • Free disk space for recordings (about 20–40 GB per camera per day of continuous HD recording)
  • On the same network as the cameras

Cameras & phone

  • IP cameras with ONVIF or RTSP (most brands)
  • Cloud-only cameras (some Ring, Arlo, Wyze) can't be added
  • Phone access: free Tailscale app on your phone and the camera PC
  • Mac version: coming soon
